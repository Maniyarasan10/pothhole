🚗 Smart Pothole Detection

ESP32 + MPU6050 rule-based road event detector

What this is

A standalone ESP32 unit mounted in a vehicle reads acceleration data from an MPU6050 at 100 Hz. A small rule engine on the device (drop / rebound / impact / jerk, with speed-bump and rough-road rejection) flags likely potholes in real time, shows the status on an onboard LCD, and serves a live dashboard over its own local WiFi network - no cloud, no internet connection required for the detector itself.
